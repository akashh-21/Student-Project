PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

LearnTrack AI
Gamified Learning, Assessment & Progress Tracking Platform
Version: 3.0 (Budget-Friendly MVP)

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
LearnTrack AI is a web-based learning platform designed to help organizations, training institutes, and learners manage learning resources, quizzes, coding assignments, and progress tracking from a single platform.

The platform combines:
- Learning Management
- Assessments
- Coding Challenges
- Progress Tracking
- Gamification
- Leaderboards
- Badges
- Learning Streaks
- Streak Recovery Challenges

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
Organizations often use multiple disconnected tools such as Excel, learning platforms, forms, coding platforms, and manual reports. LearnTrack AI provides a unified learning ecosystem.

3. PRODUCT VISION
Build an engaging, gamified learning platform where learners stay motivated through progress visibility, challenges, streaks, rewards, and healthy competition.

11. CHALLENGE ARENA (UNIQUE FEATURE)
Purpose:
A learner who loses a streak can request recovery.

Workflow:
1. Learner loses streak.
2. Learner requests recovery.
3. Admin reviews request.
4. Admin may Approve, Reject, or Issue Challenge.
5. Learner completes challenge.
6. Streak restored on success.

Challenge Types:
- Tic-Tac-Toe Challenge
- Quiz Challenge
- Coding Challenge
- Custom Challenge

Rules:
- Maximum 3 recoveries/month
- Challenge valid for 24 hours
